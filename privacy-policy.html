ScanBolt Privacy Policy Last updated: November 6, 2026 ScanBolt (package name: com.aistudio.pinkscan.pdfapp) is a document scanner app. This policy explains how the app handles your information. Information we collect: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. ScanBolt does not require an account, and it does not include ads or analytics. Camera: The app asks for camera access so you can scan documents and QR codes. Camera images are processed on your device only and are never sent to us or to any third party. Your documents: Scans, edits, and PDFs you create are saved on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. If you choose to share or export a PDF, it goes only where you send it, using your phone's share options. Internet access: The app uses a network permission only to check whether your device is online. No personal data is sent in this check. Children: ScanBolt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Changes to this policy: If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
